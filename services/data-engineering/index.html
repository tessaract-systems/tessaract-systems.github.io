Migrations

How we de-risk a migration

Re-platforming is where confidence gets lost. Four practices that keep it.

Parallel run, not a big bang
The old platform keeps serving the business while the new one is proven. Nobody is asked to trust a cutover on the strength of a test plan.
Reconciled at row level
We compare outputs row by row, not totals, and the differences get explained before anyone signs anything. "Close enough" is how trust in a platform dies.
Cost modelled before, measured after
Consumption pricing punishes bad design quietly. We model the spend up front and report actuals against it, so the invoice is never the first signal.
Your team in the room
Your engineers work alongside us through the migration. The knowledge transfers as we go, so you're not dependent on us the day after go-live.
