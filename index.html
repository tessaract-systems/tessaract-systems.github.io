1

Build your data foundation

Your data is spread across your ERP, CRM, finance tools, spreadsheets and dozens of SaaS apps. Each one tells part of the story. We bring it together on the data platform you choose, whether that's Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, BigQuery or the platform you already have, with tested pipelines and clear ownership from day one.

  • Platform selection, or a health check of the one you run today
  • Ingestion from operational systems, SaaS tools, files and streams
  • Lakehouse or warehouse modeling that mirrors how your business works
  • Data quality checks, lineage and access control built in

You get one trusted place for your company's data.

2

Give your data a shared meaning

A table can't tell anyone what an active customer is, or how net revenue is calculated. We sit with your business owners and capture those definitions, including metrics, entities, relationships and rules, in one central semantic layer. Dashboards, analysts and AI agents all read from the same source, so they all give the same answer.

  • KPI and metric definitions agreed with the people who own them
  • Semantic models in the tool that fits your stack, such as dbt, Cube, Power BI or Databricks metric views
  • A business glossary and knowledge graph that agents can query
  • Trusted dashboards and self-serve analytics on top

You get numbers everyone agrees on, and context your AI can understand.

3

Make your company AI ready

An agent is only as safe as the access you give it. Before anything goes live we set up the guardrails: which data each agent may see, which models you're comfortable using, and how every answer is evaluated, logged and costed. Your data stays in your cloud, under your security policies.

  • An AI readiness assessment and a ranked list of use cases
  • Secure tool access to your systems through the Model Context Protocol and APIs
  • Permissions, sensitive data masking and audit trails
  • A choice of models, with evaluation, monitoring and cost controls

You get a secure base that every future AI use case can build on.

4

Put specialized agents to work

Now AI can do real work. Each agent is built for one team and one job, grounded in your business semantics and connected to the tools people already use. Agents answer, draft, reconcile, route and act, and hand over to a person whenever a decision needs one.

Leadership
Answers business questions in plain language, with the numbers to back them up.

You get hours back every week, in every part of the company.
