Where we start

You don't need an AI strategy. You need one job done properly.

Almost every engagement starts with a single job, in a single team, that takes far longer than it should. Every one of these is work we have already built and run.

  • Finance

    Invoices matched, variances explained

    Month-end stops being three days of spreadsheet archaeology. Invoices match against orders on their own, and the exceptions arrive with the reason attached.

  • Customer service

    Tickets sorted, replies drafted

    First-line replies are drafted and checked before anyone opens the queue. Genuine edge cases get escalated instead of sitting for a day.

  • Reporting

    One number everyone agrees on

    The Monday rebuild stops. Finance, sales and operations pull the same figure from the same definition, and nobody spends the meeting arguing about it.

  • Sales

    Account briefs, CRM kept current

    Briefs prepared before the call rather than during it, and the CRM updated from what actually happened instead of from memory on Friday.

  • Operations

    Demand forecast, exceptions flagged early

    Forecasts that hold up after launch, and a warning when supply is about to miss — while there is still time to do something about it.

  • Leadership

    Business questions answered in plain language

    Ask in words, get an answer with the numbers behind it, without booking an analyst three days out.

Whichever one you pick, the data underneath usually needs work first — that is most of why these stall elsewhere. See how we build it
