Pricing questions

How our pricing works

Straight answers to what clients usually ask before they sign.

Why aren't prices listed?

Because the honest number depends on how many systems are involved and what state the data is in, and a figure on a page would be wrong for most readers. Tell us the shape of it on a short call and we'll give you a fixed price for the scope, in writing, before any work starts.

What isn't included in the price?

Cloud hosting, data platform licences and AI model usage. You pay those directly to the provider, so there's no markup and no lock-in. Every proposal includes an estimate of the monthly running cost.

How do payments work?

The assessment is paid upfront. Pilots are paid half at signature and half at go-live. Programs are paid in monthly milestones as each phase is delivered, and retainers are billed monthly in advance.

What happens if the scope changes?

We write a short change note with a fixed price, and nothing extra starts until you approve it. You'll never get a surprise invoice.

Which currencies do you invoice in?

US dollars, euros, British pounds and UAE dirhams, and Saudi riyals on request. Prices exclude VAT and local taxes, which are added where they apply.

Where will our data be hosted?

In your own cloud account, in the region you choose. That includes EU, UK, US, UAE and Saudi regions, so we can meet data residency rules such as GDPR and the UAE and Saudi data protection laws.

Can we skip straight to an agent?

Yes, if your data can support it. The assessment will tell you honestly. If it can't, we'll recommend the smallest foundation step that makes the agent work, rather than build something that fails on bad data.

Do you sign NDAs and data processing agreements?

Yes. We'll sign your NDA before the first detailed conversation, and a data processing agreement before we touch any personal data.

Who owns what you build?

You do. All code, configuration, prompts and documentation are yours once paid for. Your team, or another partner, can run it without us.
